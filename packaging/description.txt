A port of Gobe Productive 2.0.1, the BeOS office suite, rebuilt from the original so that it opens and saves Gobe Productive documents and works the way the original did. Everything the original does is ported: word processing (styles, headers and footers, footnotes, columns, fields, find and replace, spelling, the thesaurus), spreadsheets and charts, drawings, tables, frames, presentations and slide shows, and image processing with the painting tools, selections and the original's plug-in filters. It opens and saves Word, Excel, RTF, HTML, SYLK and text files, and pictures through the system's translators.

It needs your own copy of the original Gobe Productive 2.0 (its CD image, installer or program folder), whose menus, dialogs, strings and pictures it reads at runtime; none of them are included. It looks for it in the usual places and asks for it otherwise.

This is an unofficial reimplementation, not affiliated with the owners of Gobe Productive.
